Behind every piece is a person

Kalakart is a place to buy handmade products and support the artisans who make them. Each piece starts with someone at a wheel, a loom or a carving bench, using a craft they learned from the people before them.

We want buying handmade to be simple, and we want the makers to be seen and fairly rewarded for their work. When you shop here, you help a skill stay alive.
